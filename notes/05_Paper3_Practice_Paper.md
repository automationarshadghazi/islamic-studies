# Paper 3 — standalone practice paper

Independent author-written practice for Cambridge Islamic Studies 9488, October/November 2026 preparation. NOT an official Cambridge paper, leak or guarantee. The answer guidance is in `04_Practice_Papers_and_Answer_Guide.pdf`.

**Heritage of Islam • 1 hour 30 minutes • 50 marks**

Answer **one** question from Section A and **one** from Section B. Each question totals 25 marks: (a) 10 and (b) 15. In Section A(a), use the supplied source and your own knowledge. The text sources below are original teaching descriptions, not quotations from a medieval author or substitutes for practising genuine exam images.

### Section A — answer Question 1 OR Question 2

**Source A: an author-written description of a mosque**

A mosque occupies a site used for worship before Islam. Its courtyard opens into a large prayer hall. Reused columns and mosaic landscapes appear alongside arrangements for congregational Muslim worship. A tower makes the building visible across the city.

**1(a)** Explain how Umayyad architecture expressed Islamic belief while adapting earlier traditions. Refer to Source A and your own knowledge. **[10]**

**1(b)** “Religious policies were more important than political and economic policies to the success of the Umayyads.” Evaluate this statement. **[15]**

**Source B: an author-written account of intellectual enquiry**

A scholar studies an earlier account of vision. Rather than accepting the authority of its author, he compares the account with the behaviour of light passing through a small opening into a dark space. He records observations and uses mathematical reasoning to examine his explanation.

**2(a)** Explain how observation and reasoning contributed to scientific achievements in the Islamic world during the Abbasid era. Refer to Source B and one scientist you have studied. **[10]**

**2(b)** “Openness to other traditions was the main reason for the success of Abbasid learning.” Discuss. **[15]**

### Section B — answer Question 3 OR Question 4

**3(a)** Explain how two significant works by Muslims use different approaches to understanding Islamic beliefs. **[10]**

**3(b)** “Reasoning about the natural world is sufficient for explaining Islamic beliefs to people today.” Evaluate this statement. **[15]**

**4(a)** Explain how features of two mosques you have studied communicate Islamic beliefs. **[10]**

**4(b)** “Preserving traditional architecture is more important than creating new styles.” Discuss with reference to Muslim beliefs and communities. **[15]**
