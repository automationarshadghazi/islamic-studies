Independent author-written practice for Cambridge Islamic Studies 9488, October/November 2026 preparation. NOT an official Cambridge paper, leak or guarantee. The answer guidance is in `04_Practice_Papers_and_Answer_Guide.pdf`.

**Islam in the modern world • 1 hour 30 minutes • 50 marks**

Answer **two** questions. Each essay is worth **25 marks**, assessed as knowledge/understanding 10 and analysis/evaluation 15. Do not divide your answer into artificial (a)/(b) sections.

**1** “Muslim women can exercise public leadership without compromising their religious values.” Evaluate this statement with reference to a recent Muslim woman and women from the past whom you have studied. **[25]**

**2** “Cooperation with other faiths is the best response to the challenges faced by Muslim minorities.” Discuss with reference to two contemporary countries or communities you have studied. **[25]**

**3** “The duty to obey Muslim leaders is less important than the duty to hold them accountable.” Discuss with reference to Islamic teachings and examples of leadership. **[25]**
