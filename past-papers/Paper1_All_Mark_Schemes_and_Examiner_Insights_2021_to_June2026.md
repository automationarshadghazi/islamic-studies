## 1. Official Cambridge Examiner Annotation Codes (Used on Live Scripts)
When marking your script on screen, Cambridge Examiners stamp these exact codes on every paragraph:
- **`✓`** — Credited accurate point.
- **`EVAL`** — Explicit **evaluation / weighing / judgement** (required to unlock Level 4 & Level 5 in `1(c)`, `2(b)`, `3(b)`).
- **`TV` (Too Vague)** — You stated a generic idea (*"Muslims should be good people"*, *"Prayer helps you feel nice"*) without Islamic precision, example, or explanation -> **0 credit!**
- **`NAQ` (Not Answered Question)** — You wrote accurate facts about the general topic (e.g. listing how *zakat* is paid when the question asked *why zakat purifies*, or listing Meccan tortures when `1(b)` asked *how the Prophet reacted* to them) -> **capped at Level 1–2!**
- **`SEEN`** — Read by the examiner, repetition or off-focus, no additional credit.
- **`L1` – `L5`** — Best-fit Level awarded to the response.

## 2. Official Cambridge Marking Grids (Exact Wording Used in Every Series 2021–2026)

### Grid A: `Question 1(a)` — `[5 Marks]` (AO1 Point-Based Marking)
- **1 mark per distinct, accurate teaching** derived directly from the printed passage(s), expressed **in your own words** (do NOT copy phrases verbatim).
- Credit is also given for **named *tafsir* (commentary)** or **historical context of revelation (*asbab al-nuzul*)** where relevant.
- **Target:** Write **6 clear bullet-like sentences in continuous prose (6–8 lines total, max 7–8 minutes)** so even if two points overlap, you guarantee **5/5**.

## 3. Condensed Summary of Official Mark Scheme Answers (2021 – June 2026)

### Specimen 2021 (`9488/01`)
- **1(a) Q 10:99–100 [5]:** God knows all beliefs; if He willed, everyone on earth would believe; no compulsion in religion; belief comes only by God's will; those who reject truth remain in doubt; context of Prophet wishing uncle Abu Talib would believe.
- **1(b) Treatment of other faiths in Medina [10]:** Constitution/Charter of Medina protecting Jews, Christians, Muslims; Hadith 309 kindness to neighbours/guests; visiting sick Jewish boy; sharing food with non-Muslim neighbours; military defence only when treaties broken; model of peaceful *da'wah* without coercion (Q 2:256).
- **1(c) Free to develop own opinions today [10]:** *Agree:* No compulsion (Q 2:256), *ijtihad* for modern issues not present in 7th century, living in pluralist societies. *Disagree:* Core Qur'an & Sunnah unchanging final revelation, risk of distorting Islam; Shi'i reliance on infallible Imams rather than individual opinion.
- **2(a) Why *zakat* purifies [10]:** 2.5% *nisab* threshold; detaches heart from greed/materialism; obeys God (Q 7:156); cleanses remaining wealth and sins; builds empathy for poor and *taqwa*.
- **2(b) Recording angels main reason for *zakat* [15]:** *Agree:* *Kiraman Katibeen* record deeds for Day of Reckoning. *Disagree:* Angels are only intermediaries, not Judges; real motives are obedience/love of Allah, accountability to God directly, compassion/equality for the *ummah* ("none believes until he loves for his brother...").
- **3(a) Commitment via daily prayers [10]:** *Fajr* waking at dawn, pausing work/study 5 times daily, *wudu* & *niyyah*, repeating *Surah Al-Fatihah*, physical humility in *sujud* carrying into daily life, optional *Sunnah/Nafl* prayers.
- **3(b) Regular prayer best way for *taqwa* [15]:** *Agree:* 5 daily reminders of God, accessible to all Muslims rich or poor. *Disagree:* *Salah* can become mechanical/routine without concentration (*khushu'*); *Sawm* (Q 2:183), *Hajj* at Arafat, *Qur'an* reflection, Sufi *dhikr*, and avoiding *haram* may cultivate deeper *taqwa* depending on the individual.

### May/June 2022 (`9488/12`)
- **1(a) Q 17:34 [5]:** Protect orphan's wealth; never consume it wrongfully; touch it only to invest/improve until maturity; honour every contract/promise; God will question every covenant on Day of Reckoning.
- **1(b) Treatment of neighbours [10]:** Hadith 309 links kindness to neighbours, hospitality to guests, and good speech to faith; Hadith "not a believer whose neighbour is not safe from his harm" & "not a believer who eats his fill while neighbour goes hungry"; Prophet instructing family to share broth with Jewish neighbour; woman who prayed/fasted much but abused neighbours with tongue went to Hell.

- **3(a) & 3(b) *Nikah* performance & importance [10 + 15]:** Free consent (*ijab/qabul*), *wali*, 2 witnesses, *mahr* (dower), written/verbal contract, *khutbah*, *walimah*. *Importance for society:* Lawful channel preventing *zina*, stable family raising righteous children, emotional tranquillity (*sakinah*, *mawaddah*, *rahmah* — Q 30:21), Prophet's Sunnah vs marriage is strongly recommended rather than universally compulsory (e.g. if unable financially, or some ascetics/Sufis).
