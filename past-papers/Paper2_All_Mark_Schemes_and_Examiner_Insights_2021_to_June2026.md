## 2. Crucial Paper 2 Examiner Report Findings (`673285` Principal Examiner Report)

1. **`Part (a)` `[5 marks]` — Precision Over Waffle:** Examiners credit **1 mark per distinct accurate item** (even negative phrasing where valid, e.g., *"not praying in a graveyard/toilet"* for prayer places). However, misreading the exact noun (e.g., writing general *wudu* conditions when asked specifically about *places* of prayer, OR writing obligatory *Pillars* when asked for *habits of the Companions*) drops marks to `NAQ`. Always write **6 crisp, ultra-specific points in 4 minutes**.
2. **`Part (b)` `[10 marks]` — Name Your School of Thought Immediately:** When a question specifies *"according to **one** school of jurisprudence (fiqh) / thought"*, **state the exact school name in Sentence 1** (e.g. **Hanafi**, **Shafiʿi**, **Maliki**, **Hanbali**, or **Jaʿfari Twelver Shiʾi**). Also include **why** conditions matter (obeying Allah, following the Prophet's *Sunnah*, community unity, *ʾakhirah* reward), not just bare lists.
3. **`Compare...` Questions (`1(c)`, `2(c)`, `3(b)`, `4(b)`) — The #1 Paper 2 Trap!**
   - **Examiner Warning:** Many candidates describe Topic X for half a page and Topic Y for half a page. **Pure description of two things caps at AO1/low AO2!**
   - **What Gets Full Marks (`9–10/10` & `13–15/15`):**
     1. **Define** both terms clearly in the opening lines;
     2. Explicit **Similarities** section explaining what both share and *why*;
     3. Explicit **Differences** section contrasting where and *why* they diverge (e.g. compulsory vs recommended, Sunni vs Shiʾi, rationalist vs literalist);
     4. **Evaluative Conclusion** explicitly judging **how significant** the differences/similarities are!
4. **Naming Classical & Modern Scholars Unlocks Top Band:** The Examiner Report explicitly notes: *"Reference to quotations and scholars might be improved and was a feature of the very strongest responses."* Dropping named scholars (**Abu Hanifa, Malik, al-Shafiʿi, Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Jaʿfar al-Sadiq, al-Ashʿari, al-Ghazali, Rabiʿa al-ʿAdawiyya, Mansur al-Hallaj, Rumi, Ibn Taymiyyah, Sir Sayyid Ahmad Khan, Muhammad ʿAbduh, Fazlur Rahman**) instantly elevates scripts into **Level 4 / Level 5**!

## 3. Complete Archive of Condensed Paper 2 Mark Schemes (`Specimen 2021` – `May/June 2026`)

### Specimen 2021 (`9488/02`)
- **Q1 (Family & *ʿUrf*):** `(a)` 5 women's rights (own property, keep surname, choose/refuse spouse, receive *mahr*, inherit fixed shares, education, seek *khulʿ* divorce). `(b)` Female inheritance (`Q 4:11–12` daughter half of son's share due to male *nafaqah* duty, wife `1/4` or `1/8`, mother `1/6` or `1/3`, maternal sister equal `1/6`). `(c)` Whether *fiqh* should consider local custom (*ʿurf*) — *al-Ghazali* removing hardship, clothing/contracts vs never contradicting *Qurʾan* & *Sunnah*.
- **Q2 (Prayer Timings):** `(a)` *Fajr, Zuhr, ʿAsr, Maghrib, ʿIsha*. `(b)` Precise solar timings in one school. `(c)` Importance of *fiqh* timing differences (*ʿAsr* 1x shadow in *Shafiʿi* vs 2x in *Hanafi*; *Shiʾi* 10-min delayed sunset *Maghrib* & daily combining *Zuhr+ʿAsr* / *Maghrib+ʿIsha*; invalid early prayer & *Ramadan iftar* impact vs minor minutes when *niyyah* is sincere).
- **Q3 (*Tawhid* & Sufism):** `(a)` *Tawhid* restoring Ibrahim's monotheism, clearing Kaʿbah idols, *Shahadah* without *ʿAli* in Sunni vs with *ʿAli* in Shiʾi, rejecting *Muʾtazilah* extreme negation. `(b)` Sufi mystical practices (*dhikr*, `Q 50:16` jugular vein) vs *bidʿah* critique, *al-Hallaj* heresy risk, and clarity of *Qurʾan*/*Imams*.
- **Q4 (*Shiʾi Imams* vs *Sufi Murshid*):** `(a)` Bloodline of Muhammad via *ʿAli & Fatimah*, *Ghadir Khumm*, *ʿismah* (infallible interpretation), spiritual & legal authority. `(b)` Compare *Shiʾi Imam* (hereditary, infallible, universal source of law) vs *Sufi Murshid* (chosen spiritual guide inside a *tariqa*, guides *dhikr*, fallible human teacher).

### Oct/Nov 2021 (`9488/22`)
- **Q1 (Employment & *Qiyas*):** `(a)` Jobs avoided: selling alcohol/pork, gambling/casinos, conventional interest banking (*riba*), narcotics, weapons harming innocents, prostitution/pornography, fraud. `(b)` Employment principles: written contract, clear wage paid *"before sweat dries"*, safe humane hours, honest work, space for *Salah*, avoiding *haram*. `(c)` Usefulness of *Qiyas* today (drugs, crypto, IVF) vs *Qurʾan/Sunnah* primacy, *Ijmaʿ* safety, *Shiʾi ʿaql*, and *Hanbali/Salafi* caution.
- **Q2 (*Wudu* Occasions & Fiqh):** `(a)` Before 5 daily prayers, *Jumuʿah*, *Eid*, touching *Qurʾan*, Sufi *dhikr*, after sleep/toilet. `(b)` *Wudu* rules in one school (`Q 5:6`, Jibril teaching Prophet, *fard/mustahab/makruh*, bleeding breaks *wudu* in *Hanafi* vs NOT in *Shafiʿi*; *Shiʾi* wiping bare feet). `(c)` Importance of *wudu* differences (wiping socks *khuffayn*, hair amount wiped, single wash when pressed for time, *tayammum*).

- **Q4 (*Mahdi* & *Sunni vs Shiʾi*):** `(a)` Twelver *Mahdi* (12th Imam hidden in occultation *ghaybah*, returning with *ʿIsa* before Judgment to fill earth with justice). `(b)` Extent of differences between *Sunni* and *Shiʾi* beliefs (shared *Tawhid*, *Qurʾan*, Prophethood, 5 Pillars, *ʾAkhirah* vs *Imamah/Caliphate*, *ʿismah*, *Mahdi*, *Shahadah* addition, prayer combining & *turbah* clay tablet).
