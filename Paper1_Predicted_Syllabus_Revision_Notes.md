## 🔥 TOPIC 1: `Qurʾan 5:89–90` — Oaths & Intoxicants (`Syllabus 1.2` — #1 UNTESTED QURʾAN PASSAGE)

> 📌 **Official Syllabus Specification (`1.2`):**  
> • `5:89–90 Intoxicants` — *explain the teachings in these sources in relation to the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (pbuh); show how the teachings from these sources might be applied in the lives of Muslims today; discuss how the Prophet (pbuh) is a perfect example for Muslims to follow.*

### 📜 Exact Specified Exam Text (*A. Yusuf Ali*)
> **5:89** *Allah will not call you to account for what is futile in your oaths, but He will call you to account for your deliberate oaths: for expiation, feed ten indigent persons, on a scale of the average for the food of your families; or clothe them; or give a slave his freedom. If that is beyond your means, fast for three days. That is the expiation for the oaths ye have sworn. But keep to your oaths. Thus doth Allah make clear to you His signs, that ye may be grateful.*  
> **5:90** *O ye who believe! Intoxicants and gambling, (dedication of) stones, and (divination by) arrows, are an abomination,– of Satan’s handwork: eschew such (abomination), that ye may prosper.*

### 🧠 Complete `AO1` Revision Notes
- **Historical Context & Progressive Revelation (*Asbab al-Nuzul*):**
  - Revealed in **Medina** (*Surah Al-Maʾidah*, one of the last chapters revealed).
  - Pre-Islamic *Jahiliyyah* society was steeped in heavy wine-drinking (*khamr*), gambling debts (*maysir*), sacrificing on idol altars (*ansab*), and consulting fortune-telling arrows (*azlam*) at the *Kaʿbah* before decisions or travel.
  - **Crucial Mark-Scheme Point — The 3 Stages of Prohibiting Alcohol:** Allah banned alcohol gradually out of wisdom so deeply ingrained habits could be broken:
    1. **Stage 1 (`Qurʾan 2:219`):** Taught that wine and gambling contain *some benefit, **but their sin/harm outweighs their benefit***.
    2. **Stage 2 (`Qurʾan 4:43`):** Commanded believers **not to approach prayer (*salah*) while intoxicated** until knowing what they say.
    3. **Stage 3 (`Qurʾan 5:90–91`):** **Total, permanent prohibition** after companions asked for a decisive ruling following quarrels at drinking gatherings; when proclaimed in Medina, companions immediately smashed their clay wine jars so wine flowed in the streets.
- **Verse `5:89` — Oaths (*Ayman*) & Expiation (*Kaffarah*):**
  1. **Unintentional / Futile Oaths (*Laghw*):** Casual slips of speech (*"No, by Allah!" / "Yes, by Allah!"* spoken automatically without intent to swear a binding vow) are **forgiven without punishment**, showing Allah's mercy and realism about human speech.
  2. **Deliberate / Solemn Oaths (*Al-Ayman al-Muʿaqqadah*):** Oaths sworn consciously using Allah's Name are binding covenants; Muslims are explicitly commanded **"keep to your oaths"** (*wahfazu aymanakum*) — **unless keeping an oath involves sin**, in which case the Prophet (pbuh) said: *"Whoever swears an oath and then sees something better should do what is better and expiate his oath"* [Muslim].
  3. **Exact Expiation (*Kaffarah al-Yamin*) for Breaking a Deliberate Oath:**
     - **Step 1 (Choice of 3 equal options first):**
       - Feed **10 poor people (*masakin*)** at the **average quality of food** one feeds one's own family *(prevents giving stale/inferior leftovers!)*; **OR**
       - Clothe **10 poor people**; **OR**
       - Free **1 enslaved person** *(encouraging emancipation in early society)*.
     - **Step 2 (Only if financially unable to do ANY of Step 1):**
       - **Fast for 3 days** *(shows Islam holds poor and rich equally accountable, while turning human failure into social charity)*.
  4. **Purpose:** Clarifies Allah's signs (*ayat*) so believers cultivate **gratitude (*shukr*)**.
- **Verse `5:90` — Four Prohibited Abominations (*Rijs*):**
  1. **Intoxicants (*Khamr*):** Anything that clouds the intellect (*"Every intoxicant is khamr, and every khamr is haram"* — [Muslim]). Applied by analogy (*qiyas*) today to **illegal drugs, narcotics, and substance abuse**. Protects the **intellect (*hifz al-ʿaql*)**, family peace, health, and remembrance of Allah (`5:91`).
  2. **Gambling / Games of Chance (*Maysir*):** Acquiring others' wealth by luck without productive labour — breeds enmity, addiction, financial ruin, and hatred (`5:91`). Applied today to **casinos, lotteries, online sports betting, and speculative gambling**.
  3. **Dedication of Stones (*Ansab*):** Pagan stone altars around the *Kaʿbah* where animals were slaughtered for idols (**major *shirk*** violating *Tawhid*).
  4. **Divination by Arrows (*Azlam*):** Headless arrows marked *"Do" / "Do not" / blank* drawn by shrine custodians to claim knowledge of the unseen (*ghayb*) — replaced in Islam by **rational consultation (*shura*), *Salat al-Istikharah*, and trust in Allah (*tawakkul*)**. Modern application: **horoscopes, tarot, fortune-telling, superstitions**.
  5. **Spiritual Diagnosis & Goal:** Labelled ***rijs min ʿamal al-shaytan*** (*impure abomination of Satan's handiwork*); believers must **shun (*ijtanibu*) even approaching them** (e.g. not sitting at alcohol tables, selling, or transporting intoxicants) in order to achieve **true prosperity (*falah*)** in this world and the hereafter.
- **Link to the Prophet's Sunnah (`1.1`):**
  - Even before prophethood in Mecca, Muhammad (pbuh) **never drank wine, gambled, swore by pagan idols (*Lat / Uzza*), or consulted arrows**.
  - On *Isra wal-Miʿraj*, Jibril offered him bowls of milk and wine; he chose **milk**, representing natural purity (*fitrah*).
  - Kept every solemn covenant, including the **Treaty of Hudaybiyyah** and returning Quraish trusts via **ʿAli (ra)** at the *Hijrah*.

### ⚖️ Built-In `AO2` Evaluation Notes (`1(c)` [10 Marks])
- **Likely `1(c)` Debate Angles:** *(1) "Avoiding haram is more important than doing good deeds."* OR *(2) "Avoiding intoxicants and gambling is harder for Muslims today than in the past."*
  - **Agree Side:** Prophet's Hadith *"Avoid what I have forbidden completely; do what I have commanded as much as you are able"*; *haram* actively harms others (*huquq al-ʿibad* — drunk driving, gambling bankruptcy) and wipes out good deeds (*Hadith of the bankrupt person*); today 24/7 smartphone betting apps, workplace alcohol culture, and normalized interest/vice target youth privately.
  - **Disagree / Counter Side:** Passive avoidance alone omits the very purpose of creation (**worship, Q 51:56** & **Five Pillars, Hadith 73**); active prayer (**Q 29:45**) and fasting (**Q 2:183**) build the *taqwa* needed to resist *haram*, and good deeds erase minor sins (**Q 11:114**). Historically, early Companions lived in Meccan *Jahiliyyah* where wine and idols were the entire social economy, yet abandoned them instantaneously upon hearing `Q 5:90`.
  - **Level 5 Nuanced Conclusion:** Abstaining from grave *haram* takes precedence over **voluntary (*nafl*)** good deeds, while standing as an **inseparable partner alongside obligatory (*fard*) worship** to achieve complete *taqwa*.

## 🔥 TOPIC 2: `Qurʾan 4:12` — Inheritance (*Mirath / Faraʾid*) (`Syllabus 1.2` — CO-#1 UNTESTED PASSAGE)

> 📌 **Official Syllabus Specification (`1.2`):**  
> • `4:12 Inheritance` — *teachings in relation to the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (pbuh); application in the lives of Muslims today; how the Prophet (pbuh) is a perfect example.*

### 📜 Exact Specified Exam Text (*A. Yusuf Ali*)
> **4:12** *In what your wives leave, your share is a half, if they leave no child; but if they leave a child, ye get a fourth; after payment of legacies and debts. In what ye leave, their share is a fourth, if ye leave no child; but if ye leave a child, they get an eighth; after payment of legacies and debts. If the man or woman whose inheritance is in question, has left neither ascendants nor descendants, but has left a brother or a sister, each one of the two gets a sixth; but if more than two, they share in a third; after payment of legacies and debts; so that no loss is caused (to any one). Thus is it ordained by Allah; and Allah is All-knowing, Most Forbearing.*

### 🧠 Complete `AO1` Revision Notes
- **Historical Context (*Asbab al-Nuzul*):**
  - Revealed in **Medina** (*Surah An-Nisa*), especially after battles like **Uhud (3 AH)** left many widows and orphans (e.g. the widow of **Saʿd ibn al-Rabiʿ**, whose deceased husband's brother seized the entire estate leaving her and her two daughters destitute until the Prophet (pbuh) implemented divine shares!).
  - In pre-Islamic *Jahiliyyah*, **only adult male warriors** (*"those who ride horses and wield swords"*) inherited wealth; **wives, mothers, daughters, and young orphans inherited zero**—and widows themselves were sometimes inherited as chattel!
- **Exact Share Table inside `Qurʾan 4:12` (MEMORISE COLD — Guaranteed `5/5` in `1(a)`!):**

  | Surviving Heir | Condition | Exact Fixed Share | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Husband** (from deceased wife) | Wife leaves **NO child** (*farʿ warith*) | **`1/2` (Half)** | Reduced when children exist so children get primary support |
  | **Husband** (from deceased wife) | Wife **leaves a child** | **`1/4` (Fourth)** | Applies whether child is from him or prior marriage |
  | **Wife** (from deceased husband) | Husband leaves **NO child** | **`1/4` (Fourth)** | Shared equally if polygyny |
  | **Wife** (from deceased husband) | Husband **leaves a child** | **`1/8` (Eighth)** | Plus her *mahr* and personal property remain 100% hers! |
  | ***Kalalah* Case — Maternal Sibling** | Deceased leaves **NO parents (ascendants) & NO children (descendants)**, leaving **1 brother OR 1 sister** | **`1/6` (Sixth) EACH — EQUAL FOR MALE & FEMALE!** | **Crucial nuance for AO2:** Male and female get the **EXACT SAME `1/6` or share `1/3` equally** here! |
  | ***Kalalah* Case — Multiple Maternal Siblings** | Deceased leaves **NO parents & NO children**, leaving **more than two siblings** | **Share `1/3` (Third) equally** | Protects wider kin when nuclear line is absent |

- **Three Mandatory Pre-Distribution Rules Repeated Three Times in `4:12`:**
  1. **Funeral expenses & Debts (*Dayn*) paid FIRST** — creditors' rights (*huquq al-ʿibad*) take priority; Prophet (pbuh) stressed settling a deceased believer's debts immediately.
  2. **Legacies / Bequests (*Wasiyyah*) paid SECOND** — capped by the Prophet's Sunnah (**Saʿd ibn Abi Waqqas Hadith**) at **maximum `1/3` of the estate** (*"A third, and even a third is much; leaving heirs wealthy is better than leaving them begging"*) and **not to an existing fixed heir** so family shares aren't rigged. *(Allows Muslims today to leave up to 1/3 to non-Muslim relatives, adopted children, or mosques/charities!)*
  3. **"So that no loss/harm is caused to anyone" (*ghayra mudarr*):** Forbids fabricating fake debts or unfair wills purely to deprive rightful female/orphan heirs.
- **Divine Authority Closing `4:12`:** Ordained directly by **Allah (`Fariḍatan min Allah`)**, who is **All-Knowing (*ʿAlim*)** of true family needs across generations and **Most Forbearing (*Halim*)**, warning believers not to Delay or manipulate shares.
- **Link to the Prophet's Sunnah (`1.1`):**
  - Enforced Saʿd ibn al-Rabiʿ's widow's and daughters' shares immediately; left zero personal inheritance for his own family (*"We prophets are not inherited from; what we leave is charity"*), living simply while championing women's independent ownership (**Khadijah**), orphans' property (**Qurʾan 17:34**), and mothers' supreme honour (**Hadith 316**).

### ⚖️ Built-In `AO2` Evaluation Notes (`1(c)` [10 Marks])
- **Likely `1(c)` Debate Angle:** *"Islamic rules on inheritance / treatment of women's wealth are completely fair in the modern world."*
  - **Agree Side:** Granted women permanent, divinely protected property rights **1,200+ years before Western Married Women's Property Acts**; men receive double share in spousal/child cases **ONLY because Shariah imposes 100% legal financial obligation (*nafaqah* — housing, food, clothing, healthcare, plus marriage *mahr*) on men**, whereas a Muslim woman keeps **100% of her inheritance, *mahr*, and income for herself with zero legal obligation to spend a penny on household bills**; maternal siblings in `4:12` inherit **identically (`1/6`)** proving gender is NOT degraded, rather shares track financial duty; `1/3 wasiyyah` gives practical modern flexibility.
  - **Counter Side:** In modern dual-income economies or where brothers/husbands neglect their Islamic duty of *nafaqah* after taking double shares, divorced/single women may support entire households while receiving half the share; cultural abuse (*pressuring sisters to "forgive" their land shares*) violates `4:12` directly.
  - **Level 5 Nuanced Conclusion:** The divine formula in `4:12` is **holistically equitable when applied alongside mandatory male maintenance (*nafaqah*) and *mahr***; perceived unfairness today stems primarily from **selective practice**—men claiming their *Qurʾanic* share while abandoning their *Qurʾanic* financial duties toward female kin.

## 🕌 TOPIC 5: STRICT SYLLABUS NOTES ON `1.1 THE LIFE OF PROPHET MUHAMMAD (PBUH)`
*(Covers all 5 official syllabus sub-bullets cleanly — your universal `Q1(b)` engine!)*

1. **Bullet 1 — Early Years & First Proclamation to `622 CE`:**
   - Born **570 CE** (Year of the Elephant) in Mecca; father **ʿAbdullah** died before birth; nursed in desert by **Halimah al-Saʿdiyyah**; orphaned of mother **Aminah** at age 6, raised by grandfather **ʿAbd al-Muttalib** then uncle **Abu Talib** (worked as shepherd & honest merchant).
   - Joined ***Hilf al-Fudul*** pact protecting oppressed strangers; married **Khadijah (ra)** at 25; resolved **Black Stone (*Hajar al-Aswad*)** dispute peacefully at 35 using a shared cloak.
   - Retreated for contemplation (*tahannuth*) in **Cave Hira** on *Jabal al-Nur*; **610 CE (`Laylat al-Qadr`, age 40)**: Angel **Jibril** revealed **`Qurʾan 96:1–5`** (*"Read in the Name of thy Lord who created..."*); comforted by **Khadijah** & **Waraqah ibn Nawfal**.
2. **Bullet 2 — Challenges to Meccan Beliefs (`610–622 CE`):**
   - Directly challenged Quraish **idolatry (*360 idols* at Kaʿbah — attacking their prestige & pilgrimage revenues)**, **social arrogance/slavery** (elevating enslaved **Bilal** & **Sumayyah/Yasir**), **female infanticide**, **usury (*riba*)**, and **accountability after death**.
   - Endured mockery, physical abuse during prayer, **migrations to Christian Abyssinia (615 CE)** under just King **Negus (*Najashi*)**, **3-year economic & social boycott in *Shiʿb Abi Talib***, **Year of Sorrow (`619 CE` — deaths of Khadijah & Abu Talib)**, and bleeding stone attack at ***Taʾif*** (where he forgave and prayed for their descendants), comforted by ***Isra wal-Miʿraj* (Night Journey & Ascension — gift of 5 daily *Salah*)**, followed by **First & Second Pledges of *ʿAqabah*** with pilgrims from Yathrib.
